Bing Lets Users Turn AI Answers Off: What SEO Teams Should Know

Microsoft has introduced two ways to search Bing without AI-generated answers. Here is what changed, what remains unknown and how to keep SEO visibility checks consistent.

By Robin SEOPublished: November 16, 2026Search News & AI Search

Search results are not always presented in the same way to every user. Microsoft has now made that choice more explicit on Bing: searchers can use a preview browser extension to switch AI-generated answers on or off across their Bing searches, or add a query operator to hide AI answers for one search.

The announcement matters to SEO teams because a search visibility check can become difficult to compare if the person testing a query sees an AI answer while another sees a traditional list of links. The change does not mean websites have received a new ranking factor, and Microsoft has not published usage data showing how often people will use the controls.

What changed: In a Bing Search Blog post published November 11, 2026, Microsoft described the Microsoft Bing AI Search Choice extension in preview and a -ai query operator. The extension is available for Chrome and Edge, applies a preference across Bing searches and also sets Bing as the browser's default search engine. The operator works on an individual query.

What is the Microsoft Bing AI Search Choice extension?

Microsoft's official post, Your choice: Turn AI answers on or off in Bing, describes a browser extension that adds a toggle for whether AI-generated answers appear on Bing. The company says the extension is available for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge and that the selected preference applies across Bing searches.

There is an important installation detail: Microsoft says the extension also sets Bing as the browser's default search engine. Users should understand that change before installing it, especially if they normally use another search provider. Microsoft labels the extension as a preview and says the experience may change based on feedback.

The company also offers a no-install option. Add -ai to the end of a Bing query to request results without AI-generated answers. This is a per-query choice rather than a persistent browser preference.

What this announcement does not prove

It is easy to overstate a search-interface announcement. Microsoft's post explains the available controls, but it does not publish adoption figures, a regional usage breakdown or evidence that the controls change the organic ranking of a website. It also does not describe the extension as a ranking update.

Do not assume that a search result observed with AI answers hidden represents the experience every Bing user sees. Equally, do not assume that the presence of an AI answer means a traditional result has disappeared. The layout and available features can vary by query, market, device and user settings.

Why SEO reporting and rank checks need a consistent method

If an agency or in-house team manually checks a set of queries, it should record the conditions of each test. Otherwise, a change in the observed page could be caused by the search interface rather than a change in the website's underlying visibility.

For repeatable checks, record:

  • The search engine and exact query used
  • Country or market, language and device type
  • The date and approximate time of the observation
  • Whether AI answers were visible or hidden
  • Whether the -ai operator or browser extension was used
  • The organic URLs shown and any visible AI citations or links
  • Whether the test was signed in or used a customised browser profile

These notes do not turn a manual check into a representative audience study. They make it easier to compare like with like and explain why two screenshots may differ.

How to measure AI search visibility without relying on one screenshot

Use a combination of sources. Search Console can help measure Google's organic Search performance. Bing Webmaster Tools has also introduced an AI Performance report for publisher visibility in Microsoft AI experiences. In its June 16, 2026 announcement, Microsoft described preview capabilities for intents, topics, citation share and comparison. The company characterises citation share as an observational metric, not a ranking system or competitive scoreboard. Read the Bing Webmaster Tools AI Visibility Insights announcement for the scope and limitations.

For a business, measurement should still connect to meaningful outcomes: relevant organic visits, calls, enquiries, bookings or purchases where tracking is configured appropriately. AI mentions and citations can be useful discovery signals, but they are not the same as a qualified lead or a sale.

What small businesses and SEO teams should do now

  1. Keep a stable query set. Use a small group of searches that reflect real customer questions and commercial services.
  2. Separate AI-on and AI-off observations. Do not mix screenshots from different search experiences in one trend line without labelling them.
  3. Record sources and dates. Save the exact URL, query, market, device and test conditions when reviewing an AI answer.
  4. Check the destination pages. Make sure the pages linked from search results clearly explain the service, location, business identity and next step.
  5. Use first-party reporting. Compare manual observations with Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and analytics data, while remembering that each tool measures a different part of the journey.
  6. Avoid chasing a single interface. Useful content, accessible pages, accurate business information and clear site structure remain sensible foundations across search experiences.

Frequently asked questions

How can I turn off AI answers in Bing?

Microsoft describes two options: use the Microsoft Bing AI Search Choice browser extension in Chrome or Edge to set a preference across Bing searches, or add -ai to an individual Bing query. The extension is in preview and sets Bing as the default search engine.

Does the Bing extension change website rankings?

Microsoft's announcement describes a user-facing search preference, not a change to organic ranking systems. It does not say that installing the extension changes how a website is ranked.

Does the -ai operator work for every search engine?

No. Microsoft describes it as a Bing search operator. Do not assume it changes Google, ChatGPT or other search experiences.

Will AI-off Bing results show the same links as AI-on results?

Not necessarily. The presentation and available elements can differ. Record the search conditions and compare the actual results rather than assuming the two experiences are identical.

Can SEO tools accurately measure every AI answer?

No tool should be assumed to capture every user experience. Ask vendors how they collect results, which markets and devices they test, whether AI features are enabled and how often they repeat the checks.

Should businesses stop optimising for AI search?

No. This announcement is about giving searchers a choice on Bing. Businesses should continue making their websites useful, accessible and accurate, while measuring traditional search and AI-assisted discovery separately where data is available.
