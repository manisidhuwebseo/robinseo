FAQ

Frequently asked questions about SEO packages

Answers on pricing, contracts, results, AEO and GEO and what is included in each package.

How much do your SEO packages cost?

Packages start from $149 per month for Google Business Profile, $249 for on-page SEO, $299 for off-page SEO and $299 for AEO and GEO. The Complete Growth Pack, which combines all four, starts from $699 per month. Final pricing depends on your locations, competition and the work needed, and is confirmed after your free audit.

What is the difference between on-page, off-page and Google Business Profile SEO?

On-page SEO improves the content and structure of your own website pages. Off-page SEO builds your reputation through links, citations and brand mentions on other websites. Google Business Profile SEO improves your listing in Google Maps and local search. Most clinics need all three, which is why the Complete pack exists.

What are AEO and GEO, and do I need them?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures your pages so answer engines such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can pull a short, accurate answer. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) helps AI tools understand and recommend your business through clear details, schema, reviews and citations. They add to normal SEO and are worth it if patients or customers use AI tools to research, though no one can guarantee a mention.

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or lead numbers because Google, Bing and AI tools control what they show. We do the work that improves your chances, report on verified data and explain what is and is not working.

How long does it take to see results from an SEO package?

Google Business Profile improvements can show within a few weeks. On-page and AEO and GEO work usually takes 2 to 4 months to show, and off-page authority building often takes 3 to 6 months or more, depending on competition.

Is there a long-term contract?

Packages are billed monthly, or quarterly with a discount, and there is no long lock-in. You can pause or stop with 30 days’ notice. SEO works best as a steady, ongoing effort, so we recommend at least three to six months.

Will I keep ownership of my Google Business Profile and website?

Yes. You always own your accounts, content and data. We work through manager or editor access, and we never take ownership of your profile, domain or website.

Can I upgrade or combine packages later?

Yes. You can start with one pack and add others, or move to the Complete Growth Pack at any time. When you combine packs, the bundle discount applies from the month you upgrade.

Do you offer packages for clinics with several locations?

Yes. Each extra location needs its own Google Business Profile work, so extra locations are priced from $99 per month each on top of the GBP Pack. For five or more locations, we prepare a custom quote.

Are there hidden costs?

The starting prices cover the deliverables listed on each pack. Anything outside that scope, such as extra pages, paid tools or paid placements, is quoted and agreed before any work begins.

What do I get in the free SEO audit?

The free audit reviews your website’s technical health, your Google Business Profile, your top keywords and competitors, and your AI search readiness. You receive a prioritised action list, and there is no obligation to buy a package.
