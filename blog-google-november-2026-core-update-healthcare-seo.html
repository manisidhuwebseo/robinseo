Google November 2026 Core Update: What Healthcare Websites Need to Know

Understand the latest Google core update, how to review medical website performance, and which content, trust and technical SEO improvements healthcare businesses should prioritise.

By Robin SEOPublished: November 11, 2026Healthcare SEO

Google search algorithms continue to evolve, making search engine optimization an ongoing process for businesses, healthcare providers, and digital marketers. Every major algorithm update can influence how websites appear in search results, how users discover information, and how businesses attract organic traffic.

Google's November 2026 Core Update is an important development for website owners who depend on organic search visibility. Healthcare websites, including dental practices, medical clinics, dermatology offices, and med spas, should pay particular attention to their website performance, content quality, and overall user experience.

For healthcare businesses, search visibility is more than just website traffic. It can help potential patients discover medical services, learn about treatments, and find trustworthy healthcare providers. However, a Google core update does not automatically mean that every website will experience ranking changes. Some websites may gain visibility, while others may experience temporary or longer-term fluctuations.

In this guide, we explain what the November 2026 Core Update means, how healthcare websites can evaluate their search performance, and which SEO improvements medical practices should consider.

What Is the Google November 2026 Core Update?

A Google Core Update is a broad change to Google's search ranking systems. These updates are designed to improve the relevance and usefulness of search results for users.

Unlike updates that specifically target particular types of spam or policy violations, core updates can affect how Google evaluates and ranks content across different industries.

Google's November 2026 Core Update began rolling out on November 10, 2026. Google says the rollout applies globally and to all languages and may take up to two weeks to complete. You can follow the official status on the Google Search Status Dashboard.

Important: The rollout is still in progress as of this article's publication date. Avoid treating short-term ranking movement as the final impact. Google has not said that this update specifically targets healthcare websites.

For healthcare websites, this is a good opportunity to review important SEO factors, including:

  • Medical content accuracy and usefulness.
  • Website expertise and trust signals.
  • Page experience and mobile usability.
  • Technical SEO performance.
  • Internal linking structure.
  • Organic search traffic and keyword visibility.

The main objective should be to create useful, reliable content that genuinely helps visitors.

Why Does the Google Core Update Matter for Healthcare Websites?

Healthcare websites operate in a particularly important search category because people often use Google to research medical conditions, treatments, symptoms, and healthcare providers.

Google aims to provide helpful and reliable information, especially for topics that can affect people's health, safety, and financial well-being. Healthcare content is often associated with Google's concept of Your Money or Your Life (YMYL). This makes trust, accuracy, and expertise particularly important considerations when developing medical website content.

For example, imagine a patient searching for a dermatologist in their local area. They may want to compare available services, understand treatment options, read reviews, and find contact information. A healthcare website should make this information easy to access and understand.

1. Medical Content Quality

Healthcare articles should provide accurate, clear, and helpful information. Avoid publishing generic articles that repeat information already available on other websites without adding meaningful value. Instead, create content that answers real patient questions, explains treatments clearly, and provides useful context.

2. Author Expertise and Credibility

Medical content should have appropriate authorship and editorial oversight. Where relevant, include:

  • Author names and professional biographies.
  • Qualified medical reviewers.
  • Relevant professional credentials.
  • Reliable references.
  • Publication and review dates.

Do not claim that an article has been medically reviewed unless an appropriately qualified professional has actually reviewed it.

3. Website Trustworthiness

Patients need confidence in the healthcare provider behind a website. Make important business information easy to find, including the clinic's address, phone number, services, operating hours, and professional information. A clear About Us page and accessible contact details can also improve the visitor experience.

How to Check Whether Your Healthcare Website Is Affected

One of the biggest mistakes website owners make after a Google Core Update is immediately changing their entire SEO strategy. Instead, start by collecting data. Google Search Console is one of the most useful tools for understanding organic search performance.

Step 1: Open Google Search Console

Visit Google Search Console and select your verified website property. Open the Performance report and review:

  • Total clicks.
  • Total impressions.
  • Average click-through rate.
  • Average search position.

Step 2: Compare Search Performance

Compare your website's performance before and after the update. Remember that the November update may take up to two weeks to complete, so the initial data may not reflect its final impact. Look for patterns rather than focusing on one day of ranking changes.

Step 3: Identify Affected Pages

Review which pages experienced changes in impressions, clicks, or average position. For a healthcare website, these pages might include:

  • Dental service pages.
  • Medical treatment pages.
  • Location landing pages.
  • Educational healthcare articles.
  • Doctor and clinic information pages.

Step 4: Review Search Queries

Identify which keywords have gained or lost visibility. For example, a dental practice may monitor searches such as dentist near me, dental implants in a city, emergency dentist, cosmetic dentistry, and teeth whitening services. This information can help identify which pages deserve further investigation.

7 SEO Improvements Healthcare Websites Should Prioritize

Although there is no guaranteed method to recover or improve rankings after a core update, several SEO practices can help strengthen a healthcare website.

1. Publish Helpful and Original Medical Content

Content should address real questions from patients and provide useful information. For example, a dermatology clinic could publish articles explaining common skin treatments, preparation requirements, recovery expectations, and frequently asked questions. Avoid producing hundreds of similar location pages with little unique information. Every page should have a clear purpose and provide value to visitors.

2. Improve E-E-A-T Signals

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. Although E-E-A-T is not a single direct ranking factor, it represents important qualities Google uses in its search quality guidance. Healthcare websites should demonstrate credibility through:

  • Qualified medical professionals.
  • Accurate medical information.
  • Reliable references.
  • Clear business information.
  • Transparent authorship.
  • Genuine patient-focused resources.

3. Optimize Google Business Profile

Local SEO is especially important for healthcare businesses. A properly maintained Google Business Profile can help patients discover nearby clinics and understand the services they offer. Make sure your profile includes:

  • Accurate clinic name.
  • Correct address.
  • Updated phone number.
  • Relevant business categories.
  • Service descriptions.
  • Current operating hours.
  • Genuine patient reviews.

Never purchase fake reviews or offer misleading incentives for positive ratings.

4. Improve Website Speed and Mobile Experience

Many patients search for healthcare services using mobile devices. A website should load efficiently, display correctly on different screen sizes, and provide easy navigation. Review Google's Core Web Vitals:

  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
  • Interaction to Next Paint (INP).
  • Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

Improve image sizes, reduce unnecessary scripts, and ensure that important content is accessible without excessive scrolling or intrusive elements.

5. Strengthen Internal Linking

Internal links help visitors and search engines discover related pages. For example, a blog about dental implant treatments could link to the clinic's dental implant service page. A healthcare SEO article could also link to relevant resources about local SEO, technical SEO, and content optimization. Use descriptive anchor text that explains what visitors will find after clicking.

6. Add Relevant Structured Data

Structured data helps search engines understand information about a website and its content. Healthcare businesses may benefit from appropriate structured data, such as Organization, LocalBusiness, MedicalClinic, Dentist, Article, and BreadcrumbList. Only use schema types and properties that accurately describe the page. Structured data does not guarantee enhanced search appearances or higher rankings.

7. Update Outdated Information

Medical and healthcare information can change over time. Review older articles to identify outdated statistics, broken links, inaccurate information, and missing explanations. When an article genuinely needs updating, revise it with reliable information and clearly communicate the editorial update where appropriate. Changing the publication date without making meaningful improvements does not make an article more useful.

Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid After a Google Core Update

Healthcare website owners should avoid making unnecessary changes based on short-term ranking fluctuations. Some common mistakes include:

  • Deleting large numbers of articles: Removing content simply because it has low traffic may eliminate useful resources. Evaluate each page individually.
  • Keyword stuffing: Repeating the same keyword unnaturally can make content difficult to read.
  • Publishing AI content without editorial review: AI tools can assist with research and drafting, but medical information must be checked for accuracy and reliability.
  • Buying backlinks: Manipulative link-building practices can create search policy risks.
  • Creating duplicate location pages: Publishing nearly identical pages for multiple cities without meaningful local information can reduce content quality.
  • Changing URLs without a proper migration plan: Unnecessary URL changes can create broken links, indexing issues, and traffic losses.

How Healthcare Businesses Can Prepare for Future Google Updates

SEO should be treated as a continuous process rather than a reaction to individual algorithm updates. Healthcare businesses can establish a regular SEO maintenance routine.

Weekly SEO Tasks

  • Review Google Search Console performance.
  • Check for indexing errors.
  • Monitor important service page rankings.
  • Review website enquiries and organic conversions.
  • Check for broken links.

Monthly SEO Tasks

  • Update important healthcare articles.
  • Review local search visibility.
  • Analyze competitor content.
  • Improve internal linking.
  • Evaluate mobile usability.
  • Review Google Business Profile information.

Quarterly SEO Tasks

  • Conduct a technical SEO audit.
  • Review website content quality.
  • Evaluate important landing pages.
  • Review backlink quality.
  • Identify new content opportunities.
  • Analyze organic traffic and lead generation.

Consistency is more valuable than making large, unnecessary changes every time Google announces an update.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the Google November 2026 Core Update?

The November 2026 Core Update is a broad Google search ranking update that began rolling out on November 10, 2026. It applies globally and to all languages and may take up to two weeks to complete.

2. Can the Google Core Update affect healthcare websites?

Yes. Healthcare websites may experience changes in search visibility because core updates can affect rankings across industries. However, Google has not stated that this particular update specifically targets healthcare websites.

3. How long does a Google Core Update take to complete?

Google's announced November 2026 rollout period is up to two weeks. Website owners should monitor performance during and after the rollout rather than making immediate decisions.

4. How can I check whether my website lost rankings?

Use Google Search Console to compare clicks, impressions, average position, and affected search queries. Compare similar periods and account for seasonality and normal search fluctuations.

5. Does publishing more content improve healthcare SEO?

Publishing more content does not automatically improve rankings. Focus on creating original, accurate, helpful content that addresses genuine patient needs.

6. Is AI-generated content bad for Google rankings?

AI-generated content is not automatically against Google's guidelines. The important consideration is whether the content is useful, original, accurate, and created for people rather than solely to manipulate search rankings.

7. Should I change my entire website after a core update?

No. First investigate performance changes and identify specific problems. Avoid making drastic changes without evidence.

Conclusion

The Google November 2026 Core Update is an important reminder that successful SEO requires consistent attention to content quality, technical performance, and user experience.

For healthcare businesses, maintaining trustworthy medical information, demonstrating professional expertise, improving local search visibility, and providing a useful website experience should remain important priorities. Rather than chasing every ranking fluctuation, medical practices should invest in a sustainable healthcare SEO strategy.
