About our work

What does a Robin SEO project look like?

In short: A Robin SEO project starts with an audit of your website, Google Business Profile and competitors, then fixes the technical, local and content issues that stop patients finding and booking you. Results are tracked monthly from Google Search Console, Business Profile and call data.

We are a healthcare-focused SEO agency, so every project is built around patient searches such as “doctor near me” and “book a consultation”, not generic traffic.

We also prepare each site for AI search with clear answers and schema, so tools like Google’s AI answers can describe your clinic accurately.

How we work

How does a Robin SEO project run?

Every project follows three stages: audit, implementation, then monthly measurement and optimisation.

  1. 1

    Audit and research

    We review your site, Business Profile, tracking and the clinics competing for your patients’ searches.

  2. 2

    Fix and build

    We fix technical and local issues, improve key pages and add schema and clear answers for AI search.

  3. 3

    Measure and optimise

    We report monthly on impressions, clicks, calls and bookings, then keep improving what works.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our portfolio

Common questions about our case studies, results, AI search and working with Robin SEO.

Are these portfolio results guaranteed for my clinic?

No. Each case study shows what happened for one client over a stated period. Results vary by specialty, location, competition and starting point, and nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or patient numbers.

Where do the numbers in your case studies come from?

They come from Google Search Console, Google Business Profile and analytics reports for each project, compared before and after the work. Clinic names are kept private to protect client confidentiality.

Can you share more examples for my specialty?

Yes. Ask for an example in your specialty and city and we will share what we can, with client permission, on a short call or by email.

How long before I see results like these?

Early gains, such as a stronger Google Business Profile or faster pages, can appear within weeks. Steady growth in appointment requests usually takes 3 to 6 months, and competitive cities can take longer.

Do you work with multi-location clinics?

Yes. Each location needs its own page, Business Profile, citations and reviews so every clinic ranks in its own area while the brand stays consistent.

How does Robin SEO help with AI search (AEO and GEO)?

AEO structures pages so AI answers can quote your services and hours accurately. GEO helps AI tools understand and recommend your clinic through clear details, schema and reviews. Neither can guarantee a mention.
