Complete guide

Medical website optimisation: a complete guide for clinics

Medical website optimisation makes a clinic’s site faster, clearer and easier to find. This guide explains the main parts, in plain language.

What are Core Web Vitals and why do clinics need them?

Core Web Vitals are Google’s measures of real user experience: Largest Contentful Paint (how fast the main content appears), Interaction to Next Paint (how quickly the page responds) and Cumulative Layout Shift (how stable the layout is). Passing them gives patients a smoother visit and supports rankings.

What slows down a medical website?

The most common causes are large uncompressed images, too many plugins, heavy scripts and cheap hosting. Fixing these usually brings the biggest speed gains:

  • Compress images and serve modern formats such as WebP.
  • Remove plugins and scripts you do not need.
  • Use caching and a content delivery network.
  • Load fonts and third-party widgets efficiently.

What should a mobile clinic page include?

Show the clinic name, a tap-to-call button, a booking button and opening hours near the top. Keep text readable, buttons easy to tap and forms short, so a patient on a phone can book in under a minute.

How should patient forms handle privacy?

Use SSL on every page, collect only the information you need and use a form provider that supports HIPAA requirements. Never send sensitive health details through unprotected email.

Which medical website optimisation steps should a clinic start with?

  1. Run a speed and mobile test on your most important pages.
  2. Fix crawl errors, broken links and duplicate pages.
  3. Compress images and remove unnecessary scripts.
  4. Add clear calls to action and a short booking form.
  5. Add Physician, MedicalClinic and FAQ schema.
  6. Re-test every month and track appointment requests.

What makes a medical website easy to use?

  • Pages that load in about two to three seconds
  • Tap-to-call and booking buttons on every page
  • Plain-language headings and short paragraphs
  • High colour contrast and readable text size
  • Descriptive alt text on images
  • Clear hours, address and insurance details

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about medical website optimisation

Common questions about website speed, Core Web Vitals, schema and getting more patients from your site.

What is medical website optimisation?

Medical website optimisation is the process of improving a healthcare site’s speed, usability, structure and conversion rate so more visitors become patients and search engines can understand every page.

How long does it take to see results?

Speed and usability improvements are felt as soon as they go live. SEO benefits from those improvements typically show within 4 to 8 weeks, and larger ranking gains can take longer.

Do you redesign websites?

Yes. We offer full redesigns alongside optimisation, so your site is modern, patient-centred and built for search from the start. We can also improve your existing site without a redesign.

What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are Google’s user experience metrics: Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift. We optimise all three for your medical site.

Is my website HIPAA compliant?

We make sure any patient data handling on your site, such as forms and messages, follows HIPAA requirements. Full legal compliance also depends on your own policies, so your compliance advisor should review it.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings because search engines control them. We improve the technical and content factors within our control and report on verified data.

Does website speed affect SEO?

Yes. Page experience is a ranking factor, and slow pages also lose patients before they book. Faster pages help both search visibility and conversion.

What are AEO and GEO for a medical website?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures pages so AI answers can quote your services and hours accurately. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) helps AI tools understand and recommend your clinic through clear details, schema and reviews. Neither can guarantee a mention.
