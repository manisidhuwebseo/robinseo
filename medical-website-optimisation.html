FAQs

What is medical website optimisation?

It's the process of improving your healthcare site's speed, usability, and conversion rate to turn more visitors into patients. We target long-tail keywords like "fast loading medical website" to boost rankings.

How long does it take to see results?

Speed and UX improvements are immediate. SEO benefits from optimisation typically show within 4-8 weeks.

Do you redesign websites?

Yes, we offer full redesigns alongside optimisation to ensure your site is modern and patient-centric.

What is Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are Google's metrics for user experience, including Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), First Input Delay (FID), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS). We optimise all three for your medical site.

Is my website HIPAA compliant?

We ensure that any patient data handling on your site meets HIPAA and other relevant healthcare compliance standards.
