How to choose a healthcare SEO agency in the USA: a complete guide

Choosing a healthcare SEO agency is easier when you know what the work involves and which warning signs to avoid. This guide explains both in plain language, for practice owners and managers.

What does a healthcare SEO agency do for a US practice?

A healthcare SEO agency improves how patients find your practice online and what happens when they do. In practice, the work usually covers:

  • Improving your Google Business Profile and local map rankings.
  • Optimising service, treatment and location pages.
  • Fixing technical issues, site speed and the mobile experience.
  • Creating and reviewing patient-focused content.
  • Building trustworthy links and local citations.
  • Preparing your site for AI answers through AEO and GEO.
  • Reporting on calls, enquiries and appointments.

How is a healthcare SEO agency different from a general SEO agency?

Health is a topic where search engines apply higher standards of accuracy and trust. A healthcare specialist knows how to show clinician expertise, use reviews carefully, protect patient privacy, avoid misleading claims and focus on the local searches that drive most appointments. A general agency may treat a clinic like any other business.

What should you look for in a healthcare SEO agency?

  • Experience with healthcare websites and examples you can verify.
  • Clear monthly deliverables, not vague promises.
  • Transparent reporting on calls, enquiries and rankings.
  • Ethical link building, without spam or private networks.
  • Awareness of medical advertising rules and patient privacy.
  • Full ownership of your accounts, website and data.

Which red flags should you avoid when hiring a healthcare SEO agency?

  • Guaranteed number one rankings or guaranteed patient numbers.
  • Cheap packages that never explain the work.
  • Links bought from private blog networks.
  • Long lock-in contracts with no way out.
  • No access for you to your own Google profile or analytics.
  • Reports full of vanity metrics but no calls or bookings.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about hiring a healthcare SEO agency

Common questions about healthcare seo agency services, AI search and getting more patients.

What services does Robin SEO provide as a healthcare SEO agency?

Robin SEO provides Google Business Profile and local SEO, on-page and off-page SEO, technical SEO and site speed, content and E-E-A-T, and AEO and GEO for AI search, with monthly reporting on calls, enquiries and rankings.

Which types of practices do you work with?

We work with dentists, dermatologists, med spas, chiropractors, physical therapists, plastic and hair restoration surgeons, eye clinics, primary care practices and mental health providers, as well as multi-location groups.

Do you work with practices across the whole USA?

Yes. We work remotely with practices in cities across the United States and tailor keywords, pages and local signals to each market, from Houston and Dallas to New York and Seattle.

How long does healthcare SEO take to work?

Local improvements, such as a stronger Google Business Profile, can show within weeks. Steadier growth in patient enquiries usually takes 3 to 6 months, and competitive cities and specialties can take longer.

Do you guarantee rankings or patient numbers?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or patient numbers because search engines and patient behaviour vary. We improve the factors within our control, report on verified data and explain what is and is not working.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

Our plans are billed monthly or quarterly with no long lock-in, and you can pause or stop with 30 days’ notice. SEO works best as a steady effort, so we recommend at least three to six months.

Will I keep ownership of my accounts and website?

Yes. You always own your Google Business Profile, website, analytics and content. We work through manager or editor access and never take ownership of your accounts.

Can you work with my current website developer or marketing team?

Yes. We often work alongside existing developers and marketers. We document exactly what to change, agree who does what and check the results afterwards.

How do you report results?

You receive a clear monthly report covering rankings, Google Maps views, calls, form and WhatsApp enquiries, and where you share the data, booked appointments, with plain explanations and next steps.

What are AEO and GEO for a healthcare practice?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures pages so AI answers can quote your services, hours and details accurately. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) helps AI tools understand and recommend your practice through clear details, schema and reviews. Neither can guarantee a mention.
