Complete guide

Healthcare local SEO: a complete guide for clinics

Healthcare local SEO helps nearby patients find your clinic in Google Maps and local search. This guide explains the main parts, in plain language.

How do clinics rank in the Google Maps pack?

Google weighs three things for local results: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how close you are to the searcher) and prominence (how well known and trusted you are). You cannot change distance, but you can improve relevance and prominence.

What should a Google Business Profile include?

A complete profile gives Google and patients everything they need. Strong profiles usually include:

  • The exact clinic name, address and phone number, matching your website.
  • A primary category and relevant secondary categories.
  • Services, opening hours and a booking link.
  • Real photos of the clinic, team and reception.
  • Regular posts and answers to common patient questions.

Why do local citations and consistent details matter?

A citation is a mention of your clinic’s name, address and phone number on another site. When these details match everywhere, search engines trust your location more. Inconsistent details can hold back your map ranking.

How do you get more patient reviews ethically?

Ask every happy patient for a review, make it easy with a direct link, and reply to every review professionally. Never offer incentives or post fake reviews, and never reveal patient health information in a reply.

How do multi-location clinics rank in every area?

Each location needs its own page, Google Business Profile, citations and reviews. Share the specialists and services that are genuinely available at each site, rather than copying the same text across pages.

Which local SEO steps should a clinic start with?

  1. Claim and verify your Google Business Profile.
  2. Make your name, address and phone number consistent everywhere.
  3. Build a page for each location and service.
  4. Ask patients for reviews and reply to them all.
  5. Add local schema to your website.
  6. Track map pack rankings and calls every month.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about healthcare local SEO

Common questions about Google Maps, Business Profiles, reviews and getting more nearby patients.

What is healthcare local SEO?

Healthcare local SEO is the process of optimising your online presence, including your Google Business Profile, listings, reviews and location pages, to attract more patients from local searches and Google Maps.

How long does local SEO take?

Early improvements, such as a stronger Google Business Profile, can appear within 4 to 6 weeks. Significant growth in map rankings and patient calls usually takes 3 to 4 months, and competitive cities can take longer.

Do you work with multi-location clinics?

Yes. Each location needs its own page, Google Business Profile, citations and reviews, so every clinic can rank in its own area while the brand stays consistent.

What are local citations?

Local citations are mentions of your clinic’s name, address and phone number on other websites and directories. Consistent citations build trust with search engines and boost local authority.

How important are Google reviews?

Reviews are a major local ranking factor and strongly influence which clinic a patient chooses. We help you earn genuine reviews ethically and never use fake or incentivised reviews.

Do you guarantee a #1 map ranking?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee map pack positions because Google controls them. We improve the factors within our control, report on verified data and explain what is and is not working.

Can you fix wrong or duplicate listings?

Yes. We find and correct inaccurate or duplicate listings for your clinic, so patients and search engines see one consistent set of details.

What are AEO and GEO for a clinic?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures pages so AI answers can quote your services and hours accurately. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) helps AI tools understand and recommend your clinic through clear details, schema and reviews. Neither can guarantee a mention.
