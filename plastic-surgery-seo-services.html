Complete guide

Plastic surgery SEO services: a complete guide for surgeons

Plastic surgery SEO services make a practice easy to find, easy to trust and easy to contact when a prospective patient searches for a procedure or a surgeon. This guide explains how it works, in plain language, for surgeons and practice managers.

How do patients search for a plastic surgeon?

Patients search in two stages. First they research a procedure, with questions such as what it involves, how long recovery takes and what it costs. Later they search for a surgeon, usually with a location, such as “facelift surgeon in Miami”. Your website needs pages that answer the first stage and rank well for the second.

What should a procedure page include to rank?

A procedure page ranks when it answers a patient’s questions clearly and shows that a qualified surgeon stands behind the information. The strongest pages usually include:

  • A heading and title that name the procedure and the city, for example “Rhinoplasty in Houston”.
  • A short, direct explanation of what the procedure is and who may be a candidate.
  • The consultation process, recovery overview and general risks, written in balanced language.
  • The surgeon’s qualifications, board certification where accurate and hospital affiliations.
  • Honest before-and-after images with permission and context, where the practice uses them.
  • Frequently asked questions, structured data and one clear way to request a consultation.

How should before-and-after photos be used on a plastic surgery website?

Before-and-after photos should be used with written patient consent, consistent lighting and angles, and a note that results vary. For SEO, give each image a descriptive file name, useful alt text and a short caption. Never edit photos misleadingly or imply that a particular result is guaranteed.

How does SEO help a surgeon build trust online?

SEO builds trust by making credentials, experience and real patient feedback easy for search engines and visitors to find. Google holds health pages to a higher standard, so we show who the surgeon is, where they trained, which procedures they perform and how patients can verify that information.

Which plastic surgery SEO services should a practice start with?

  1. Fix technical basics so Google and Bing can crawl and index every procedure page.
  2. Complete the Google Business Profile with correct categories, photos and services.
  3. Build or improve one page for each procedure the surgeon actually performs.
  4. Collect reviews from willing patients and respond to each one.
  5. Publish helpful consultation and recovery guides, reviewed by the surgeon.
  6. Earn citations and links from relevant medical and local websites.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about plastic surgery SEO

Common questions about plastic surgery seo services, AI search and getting more patients.

What is included in plastic surgery SEO?

Plastic surgery SEO includes keyword research for procedures and locations, procedure page optimisation, surgeon credibility content, local SEO and Google Business Profile work, technical fixes, schema markup and monthly reporting on rankings and consultation requests.

Can SEO help a plastic surgeon attract procedure-specific enquiries?

Yes. Dedicated pages for each procedure, such as rhinoplasty or breast augmentation, can rank for specific searches and attract people who already know what they want to research. The pages must be accurate, balanced and written around procedures the surgeon actually performs.

Are before-and-after photos important for SEO?

They can help patients and add useful content, but they must be used with written consent and honest context. For SEO, use descriptive file names, alt text and captions. Photos never replace helpful written information about the procedure.

Should procedure pages discuss risks?

Yes. Responsible procedure pages explain general risks, recovery considerations and the consultation process in balanced language. This builds trust with patients and shows search engines that the content is careful and expert.

Do you guarantee consultation volume or rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings or consultation numbers because search engines control rankings. We improve the factors within our control, report on verified data and explain what is and is not working.

How long does plastic surgery SEO take to show results?

Early improvements can appear within 4 to 8 weeks, but meaningful growth in consultation requests usually takes 3 to 6 months. Highly competitive cities, such as Los Angeles or Miami, can take longer.

Can you rank my practice in several cities and suburbs?

Yes. We target your main city plus the suburbs and neighbourhoods patients travel from, using location-specific keywords, Google Business Profile work and local citations. Each location should have a genuine address or service area so the rankings are real.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) for plastic surgeons?

AEO structures your pages so answer engines, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, can pull a short, accurate answer about your procedures. It uses question-style headings, direct answers, FAQ schema and consistent practice details.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) for plastic surgeons?

GEO increases the chance that generative AI tools understand, mention and recommend your practice. It relies on clear entity details, schema markup, reviews, citations and trustworthy content. No agency can guarantee a mention.
