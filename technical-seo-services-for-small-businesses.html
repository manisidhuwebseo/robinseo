Technical SEO explained

What is technical SEO?

In short: Technical SEO is the work of making a website easy for search engines to crawl, render, index and understand, while keeping it fast, secure and easy to use on mobile, so that its content can rank on Google, Bing and AI answer engines.

Your website can have useful content and great service, yet technical problems may stop search engines from finding or trusting important pages. Slow pages, duplicate URLs, broken redirects and blocked resources are common causes.

Our technical SEO services find those issues, rank them by impact and fix them in a sensible order. We also target long-tail searches such as “fix pages not indexed by Google” or “technical SEO audit for small business” on this page.

Complete guide

Technical SEO services for small businesses: a complete guide

Technical SEO services make sure the foundations of your website are sound, so your content and links can do their job. This guide explains the main problems, in plain English, for business owners who are not developers.

Which technical SEO problems hurt small business websites most?

The problems that do the most damage are the ones that stop pages being found or trusted. The most common are:

  • Important pages that are not indexed, or that are blocked by robots.txt or noindex tags.
  • Slow pages caused by large images, heavy scripts or poor hosting.
  • Duplicate URLs created by http and https, www, trailing slashes or tracking parameters.
  • Broken links, redirect chains and incorrect status codes.
  • Missing or incorrect structured data.
  • Pages that are hard to use on a phone.

What is the difference between crawling and indexing?

Crawling is when a search engine visits a page, and indexing is when it decides to store that page so it can appear in results. A page can be crawled but not indexed, which is why a technical review checks both. Fixing crawl access is usually the first step.

Do Core Web Vitals affect rankings?

Core Web Vitals measure loading speed, responsiveness and visual stability, and Google uses page experience as one of many signals. They are rarely the only reason a page ranks, but a fast, stable page keeps visitors engaged and helps conversions. Aim for good scores on your most important pages first.

Does technical SEO help with AI search visibility?

Yes. AI search tools rely on pages they can crawl, read and understand. Clean HTML, accessible content, correct robots rules and accurate structured data make it easier for answer engines to use your pages. No one can guarantee that an AI tool will mention your business.

What should a small business fix first?

  1. Check that Google can index your key pages, using Search Console.
  2. Fix the pages returning errors and any redirect chains.
  3. Set a single preferred version of each URL with canonical tags.
  4. Improve speed on the pages that bring in enquiries.
  5. Submit a clean XML sitemap and review robots.txt.
  6. Add schema where it matches visible content.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about technical SEO

Common questions about technical seo services, AI search and getting more customers.

What is included in technical SEO services?

Technical SEO services include crawl and indexation checks, XML sitemap and robots.txt review, canonical and redirect checks, broken links, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals and speed opportunities, schema validation and internal linking review.

How do I know if my website has technical SEO problems?

Warning signs include important pages missing from Google, slow loading on phones, errors in Search Console, duplicate URLs and a drop in traffic after a redesign. A technical review checks these against your actual site.

Can you fix technical SEO issues on WordPress, Shopify or Wix?

Yes. We work with common platforms, though each has its own limits. Where a fix needs a developer or a platform setting, we document exactly what to change and check it afterwards.

Do Core Web Vitals guarantee better rankings?

No. Core Web Vitals are one of many signals, and a fast page does not guarantee a ranking. They do improve user experience, which supports engagement and enquiries.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings because search engines control them. We fix what is within our control, validate the changes and report on verified data.

How long does technical SEO take to show results?

Fixes that unblock crawling and indexing can show within days to a few weeks after search engines revisit the pages. Ranking and traffic improvements usually take longer, often 2 to 3 months.

Do you work with businesses in the UK as well as the USA?

Yes. We support small businesses in the United States and the United Kingdom remotely, and we adjust recommendations to your market and search engines.

What are AEO and GEO, and does technical SEO help?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) aim to make your content easy for AI answers to use. Technical SEO helps by keeping pages crawlable, structured and accurate. Nothing can guarantee that an AI tool will mention your business.
