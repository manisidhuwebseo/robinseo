When technical SEO is needed

Is Your Website Losing Search Visibility Because of Technical Issues?

Technical SEO is not about adding keywords to every page. It is about making sure search engines can access important URLs, understand how your pages relate to one another and deliver a usable experience to visitors.

01

Important pages are not indexed

Service pages or new content may be missing from search results. We investigate crawl access, index directives, canonicals, redirects and internal links to find likely causes.

02

Your website loads slowly

Heavy images, scripts, fonts or inefficient layouts can make pages feel slow. We review performance opportunities and prioritise changes that matter to users.

03

Google finds duplicate URLs

HTTP/HTTPS, trailing slashes, tracking parameters and duplicate page versions can create confusion. We review canonical and redirect signals for consistency.

04

Search Console reports errors

Coverage, sitemap, mobile and enhancement reports can reveal problems. We interpret the reports in the context of your actual website instead of treating every warning equally.

05

Your site structure is difficult to crawl

Important pages buried too deeply or missing internal links can be harder to discover. We assess navigation, page hierarchy and contextual links.

06

A redesign or migration affected SEO

Changed URLs, missing redirects or lost page content can affect organic visibility. We review URL mapping, redirects, canonicals and post-launch checks.

Common questions

Technical SEO Services: Frequently Asked Questions

What do technical SEO services for small businesses include?

They can include crawlability and indexation checks, sitemap and robots.txt review, canonical and redirect checks, broken-link discovery, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals, structured data and internal-linking improvements. The exact scope depends on your website.

Can technical SEO help pages that are not indexed by Google?

A technical review can identify possible causes such as crawl blocks, incorrect noindex directives, canonical conflicts, redirect problems, weak internal links or server errors. Fixing an issue does not guarantee indexing because Google makes its own indexing decisions.

Do you fix technical SEO issues or only provide an audit?

The project can include an audit, prioritised recommendations and implementation support. The exact work depends on your platform, the access available and the issues found. We agree the scope before work begins.

How long does a technical SEO audit take?

It depends on website size, platform and complexity. A small business website can usually be reviewed more quickly than a large ecommerce or multi-location site. We confirm the expected timeline after understanding your website.

Can you work with businesses in the USA and UK?

Yes. Robin SEO can discuss remote technical SEO projects with businesses in the United States and United Kingdom. Scope, communication and working arrangements are agreed before work begins.

Will technical SEO guarantee first-page rankings?

No ethical SEO provider can guarantee a specific ranking. Technical SEO helps remove obstacles and improve how a website can be crawled, understood and used. Rankings also depend on relevance, competition, content quality, authority and other factors.
