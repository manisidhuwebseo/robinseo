FAQ

Questions clinics ask before starting SEO.

What is healthcare SEO?

Healthcare SEO is the process of improving a medical website so patients can find it on Google when they search for treatments, doctors and clinics. It covers technical fixes, service pages, local SEO, content and trust signals.

Do you work with clinics across the USA?

Yes. We plan campaigns around US cities and states, so a dentist in Houston and a med spa in Miami each get a strategy based on their own local search demand.

Which specialties do you support?

Dental and orthodontic, dermatology, med spa, hair transplant, chiropractic, physical therapy, plastic surgery, ophthalmology, primary care and mental health practices, plus technical SEO for small businesses.

Can SEO help my clinic get more patient enquiries?

SEO helps patients who are already searching for your services find you. Results depend on competition, location, website experience, trust and how enquiries are handled.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is long-term. Technical and local improvements can show movement sooner, while competitive keywords usually take several months of consistent work.

Is local SEO different from regular SEO?

Yes. Local SEO focuses on “near me” searches, Google Maps, your Google Business Profile, reviews and location pages. Regular SEO focuses on organic rankings for your website pages. Most practices need both.

What is AI search optimisation?

It means structuring your content, schema markup and practice information clearly, so AI answer engines and Google AI features can understand and reference your practice.

What does a healthcare SEO agency do?

A healthcare SEO agency improves how a clinic appears in Google Search, Google Maps and AI answers. That includes technical fixes, service and location pages, Google Business Profile optimisation, reviews, schema markup and tracking patient enquiries.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) for clinics?

AEO means writing and structuring your pages so answer engines, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, can pull a short, accurate answer about your practice. It uses question-style headings, direct answers, FAQ schema and consistent practice details.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)?

GEO is about increasing the chance that generative AI tools understand, mention and recommend your practice. It relies on clear entity information (name, address, specialties), schema markup, reviews, citations and helpful, trustworthy content. No agency can guarantee a mention.

How can my clinic appear in AI answers like ChatGPT and Gemini?

Keep your name, address and phone consistent everywhere, add structured data, publish clear answers to the questions patients ask, earn genuine reviews and make sure your Google Business Profile is complete. AI tools tend to reference practices they can clearly identify and trust.

Do you guarantee first-page or number one rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings because Google controls them. We focus on the work that improves your chances, report on verified data and explain what is and is not working.
